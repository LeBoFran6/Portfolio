FittingBox — Virtual Try-On

Virtual try-on deployed to web and mobile for international eyewear brands, under hard bandwidth and device constraints.

FittingBox02/2024 – 06/2024professionaloptimisationenvironment
Role
3D Designer — Real-Time Product Visualisation
Period
02/2024 – 06/2024
Context
Commercial real-time 3D product visualisation
Constraint
Runs in a browser and on mobile — bandwidth, draw calls and device spread
Output
300+ real-time assets per month

The problem

Luxury eyewear brands needed virtual try-on that a customer could actually use — in a browser, on a phone, on whatever connection they happen to have. The models have to read as premium at close range while staying inside a payload a page can load without the customer leaving.

What I did

1
300+ real-time assets per monthCreated and optimised the asset volume the try-on catalogue needed, sustained as a monthly cadence rather than a one-off push.
2
Integration into proprietary real-time pipelinesFed large asset datasets into tooling built for the web and mobile constraints rather than desktop rendering.
3
Optimisation under strict budgetsHeld material and geometry cost down so the result stayed interactive on the weakest device in the supported spread.

Result

Shipped try-on experiences used by international eyewear brands on their live storefronts — real-time 3D delivered where the customer already is, not in a dedicated viewer.
