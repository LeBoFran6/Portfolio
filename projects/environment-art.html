World Machine in production

I learned World Machine at Eugen Systems and wanted to make it the backbone of a personal project. My methodology was iteration: first the basic shape, blended and extended into another, then each detail layer in turn — distortion, wind, waves — until the result held up.

Every step of the creation is visible in the sequence below, from raw heightfield to final dressed environment.

Bringing it into engine

Once in Unreal I implemented the landscape and populated the location with desert assets. The difficulty with deserts is finding the right amount of decoration against the right amount of open space — too little and it reads as empty, too much and it stops reading as a desert at all.
